Website Project Plan

Subject: Professional Personal Portfolio | WDD 131

1. Site Name

Proposed Name: Iwu Christopher Portfolio

Reasoning: Using my own name puts personal identity and accountability front and center for recruiters, hiring managers, and freelance clients looking for reliable work and clear communication.

2. Site Purpose

This website serves as a central online hub presenting my background, technical and creative skills, and completed projects. It is organized into four core sections:

  • Home / Landing Page: A concise introduction highlighting who I am, what I build, and quick navigation to my work and contact options.
  • About Me: Details on my professional background, learning journey, interests, and career goals.
  • Projects / Work: A curated showcase of key projects, coursework, and creative builds with descriptions and live or repository links.
  • Contact: A contact form and direct links to professional profiles (such as GitHub and Interstride) along with social channels (such as Facebook).

3. Scenarios

Questions from prospective employers, collaborators, or clients that shape the content on the site:

  • What projects have you built recently, and can I inspect the code or live demos to judge your problem-solving and layout skills?
  • What is your background, core skill set, and career direction, and how do you approach new technical challenges?
  • How can I reach you directly for an opportunity, or check your profiles on GitHub, Interstride, and Facebook?

4. Color Schema

Inspired by a minimal neutral aesthetic, this palette relies on dark surfaces, subtle gray borders, and crisp light neutrals for a clean, distraction-free portfolio feel[cite: 1].

Shadow Black #101014 Primary background for the page canvas, header, footer, and dark surface blocks[cite: 1].
Slate Gray #3d3d47 Used for subtle card borders, pills/badges, dividers, and secondary UI accents[cite: 1].
Light Gray-Blue #d0d1db Used for headings, primary buttons/highlights, and key emphasis text against dark backgrounds[cite: 1].
Warm Gray #e6e6e6 Used for body copy, descriptions, and list items to keep reading comfortable on dark backgrounds[cite: 1].

5. Typography

Heading Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Semi-Bold / Bold)

Application: Applied to the page title, section headings, and card titles to give a crisp, modern editorial hierarchy.

Body Font: Inter (Regular / Medium / Semi-Bold)

Application: Applied to body text, bullet points, captions, and form/link descriptions for clear legibility at small and large sizes.
